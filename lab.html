Analyze three months of sample orders for an online store.

Online store mini-project

Dataset schema

The orders table has one row per product line. An order ID can appear on multiple rows.

order_id
Order number
order_date
Date in YYYY-MM-DD format
customer
Customer name
product
Product name
quantity
Units purchased
unit_price_cents
Price per unit in cents

The lab uses SQLite. Results are graded by row values, column order, and row order.
